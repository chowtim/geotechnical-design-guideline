# Design

The note is a document read at a desk under office light. The page is light paper and near-black ink.

## Frame

The sticky bar is an ink title strip. It carries the name Suggested Design Guideline and the three procedure links. The current procedure is paper on that ink. Good practice is a text link in the same strip, not a fourth procedure button.

## Column

Body text is the ink, in Segoe UI, about 17px, on a measure near 68 characters. Tables run wider than the prose and use tabular figures. Section numbers stay in the headings because the draft is cited that way.

## Mark

Yellow is the amendment mark from the draft's own good-practice note. It is used on the incomplete wall-friction sentence and on text selection. It is not a second brand colour for buttons.

## Motion

A procedure jump does not animate. A bar link scales to 0.97 on press, transform only, 160ms, with a strong ease-out. Reduced motion drops the scale and uses opacity. Hover styles apply only for a fine pointer.
