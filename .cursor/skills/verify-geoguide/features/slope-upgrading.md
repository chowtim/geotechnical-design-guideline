# Soil nail design

Soil nail design covers nails, drainage, and crest structures from the draft. The bar label is `Soil nail design`.

## Sub-features

- `slope-open` reaches the procedure from the bar.
- `slope-nails` reaches the soil-nail steps.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `Soil nail design` in the top bar.
- Choose `3.2 Key design steps for soil-nailed slopes`.

## Driving it with the Cursor browser

Preconditions:

- Doctor passed.
- The note was opened with no hash.

- **Open the procedure.** Choose `Soil nail design`. Click `a[data-procedure="soil-nail"]`. The hash is `#soil-nail`. The heading starts with `3. Soil nail design`. That link is current. The heading is below the bar.
- **Open the nail steps.** Choose `3.2 Key design steps for soil-nailed slopes`. The hash is `#slope-nails`. The table is Geoguide 7 Table 5.4. Economic consequence A is 1.4, 1.4, and 1.4.
- **Proof.** Record both hashes and the category A row. The ELS table also contains factors of safety. Quote the soil-nail table, not the ELS table.

## Gotchas

- The bar name is `Soil nail design`, the same words as the heading.
- Section 3.2 is inside this procedure. Do not click a `2.2` link from ELS.
