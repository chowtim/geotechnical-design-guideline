# Desk study

Desk study is the first procedure. It lists the objectives, the sources to review, the outputs, and where the findings sit in the report.

## Sub-features

- `desk-open` reaches the procedure from the bar.
- `desk-sources` reaches the source checklist from the links under the procedure heading.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `Desk study` in the top bar.
- Under the heading, choose `1.2 Standard sources to review (minimum checklist)`.

## Driving it with the Cursor browser

Preconditions:

- Doctor passed.
- The note was opened with no hash.

- **Open the procedure.** Choose `Desk study`. Click `a[data-procedure="desk-study"]`. The hash is `#desk-study`. The heading `1. Desk study` is below the bar.
- **Open the checklist.** Choose `1.2 Standard sources to review (minimum checklist)`. Click that link. The hash is `#desk-study-sources`. The heading of that section is below the bar. The list includes `Slope Information System (SIS)`.
- **Proof.** Record both hashes and both headings. A landing that shows only the procedure heading does not prove the checklist link.

## Gotchas

- The standards list near the top of the page is not the desk-study source checklist.
- The section link is under the Desk study heading. A link with a similar name inside another procedure is the wrong one.
