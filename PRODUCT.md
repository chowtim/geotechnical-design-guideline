# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no build step and no deploy. Confirmed by the approved study-note plan the user ordered built. The note opens from a local static server and from the file itself.

## Users

A young geotechnical engineer in Hong Kong, at a desk, using the note while preparing or checking a submission. They already work in the language of Geoguides, GEO publications, and Buildings Department practice notes.

## Product Purpose

Give that engineer a non-official, step-by-step reading of one draft note, and a control that opens Desk Study, excavation and lateral support, or soil nail design in one action. Success is that the engineer can reach the procedure and read the draft's own checks without hunting.

## Positioning

The note is a transcription of one attached draft, with the draft's incomplete wall-friction phrase left incomplete. A generic geotechnical textbook could not truthfully claim that limit.

## Operating Context

Local reading in a browser. The source file stays in the project as `edited_Design Procedures for Various Geotechnical Works.docx`. The procedures are desk study, ELS, and soil nail design for public housing and similar Hong Kong work. No accounts, no server data, no submission workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site carries the draft's standards list, scheduled-area table, three procedures, and good-practice notes. It does not add factors of safety, software steps, or design methods the draft does not state. It is not GEO, CEDD, or Buildings Department guidance. Language is English. Deployment is undecided and out of scope.

## Brand Commitments

Suggested design guideline. The draft's real title remains the page title. Section numbers stay because they are how the draft is cited.

## Evidence on Hand

The draft docx named above. No photographs, no project logos, and no permission to present archive pictures as this note's sites. Do not invent quotations, project statistics, or department endorsements.

## Product Principles

Transcribe the draft. Mark gaps instead of filling them. Keep each procedure one action away. Prefer a document a checker can scan over a page that performs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access to every jump. The heading reached by a jump stays clear of the sticky bar. Body text meets ordinary reading contrast. Reduced motion keeps the note still.
